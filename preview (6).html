Growth play

Existing Client Opportunity Mining

Systematically identify policy reviews, term conversion windows, estate-liquidity needs, business-owner coverage gaps, premium-financing reviews, and life-event triggers already inside Balefire relationships.

Growth play

Advisor Activation

Make insurance expertise easy to bring into Wealth Advisory and Planning conversations. Give advisors recognizable insurance triggers, quick consultation access, and closed-loop follow-up so asking for help creates value rather than work.

Growth play

Corporate Solutions Bridge

Mine business-owner relationships for succession, buy-sell, key-person, executive benefit, retention, and personal planning opportunities that naturally connect Corporate Solutions and insurance expertise.

Growth play

COI Growth Network

Build repeatable relationships with estate attorneys, CPAs, bankers, M&A professionals, and other centers of influence around real planning problems—not product pitches.

Growth play

Education-to-Opportunity

Use targeted roundtables, advisor sessions, client education, and business-owner conversations to surface needs. Every initiative has a named growth owner and a defined follow-up path.

Growth play

90-Day Growth Experiments

Use focused growth experiments to test new channels, measure results, scale what works, and retire what does not.

Growth-to-delivery framework

Closed-Loop Insurance Growth Model

The growth team owns opportunity creation, strategy, and relationship continuity. Christina's team owns delivery. Steven resolves cross-division barriers and protects the handoffs.

1

Create Demand

Generate an insurance conversation through existing clients, advisor partnerships, COIs, business-owner needs, events, planning triggers, market relationships, and original growth ideas.

Primary owner: Insurance Organic Growth
2

Shape the Opportunity

Understand the need, create momentum, bring in the right expertise, and determine the client path without turning the process into administrative stage management.

Primary owner: Insurance Organic Growth
3

Lead the Strategy

Coordinate the client conversation, planning context, technical expertise, and recommendation so the opportunity has a clear reason to move forward.

Primary owner: Insurance Organic Growth
4

Commit

Client and relationship team align on the path forward, responsibilities, timing, and what Client Engagement needs to execute successfully.

Primary owner: Insurance Organic Growth
5

Transition

Christina's team receives a complete, executable case. Steven steps in where priorities, resources, complexity, or cross-team friction could interrupt momentum.

Owners: Insurance Organic Growth + Insurance Client Engagement • Steven supports the handoff
6

Deliver

Application, underwriting, requirements, carrier coordination, implementation, communication, and documentation move through the Insurance Client Engagement team.

Primary owner: Christina + Delivery team
7

Place & Serve

Complete implementation, confirm the client experience, establish service ownership, and surface anything that should return to the advisory conversation.

Primary owner: Insurance Client Engagement
8

Expand

The Insurance Growth Team returns to the relationship: review what changed, identify the next planning need, ask for introductions when appropriate, and create the next opportunity.

Primary owner: Insurance Organic Growth • Loop restarts
